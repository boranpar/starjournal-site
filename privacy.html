Star Journal kişisel bir günlüktür. Hesap yoktur, giriş yoktur, reklam yoktur. Yazdıklarını biz göremeyiz.

Uygulamada kullanım analizi, çökme raporu ya da reklam aracı kullanmıyoruz. Bu sayfa aynı zamanda 6698 sayılı Kişisel Verilerin Korunması Kanunu'nun (KVKK) 10. maddesindeki aydınlatma metnidir: neyin nereye, neden gittiğini ve haklarını burada bulursun.

Yazdıkların

Başlık, metin, etiketler, his ve eklediğin fotoğraflar kaydedilmeden önce iPhone'unda AES-GCM ile şifrelenir. Anahtar senin cihazında ve iCloud Anahtar Zinciri'nde durur; bizde bir kopyası yoktur. Eşitleme açıksa şifreli veriler yalnız kendi iCloud hesabının özel veritabanına gider. Kurtarma anahtarını kaybedersen yazdıklarını biz de açamayız.

Tek istisna Anı widget'ıdır: uygulama kilidi kapalıyken, ana ekrandaki Anı widget'ı geçmiş bir günün başlığını, ilk satırlarını ve küçültülmüş bir fotoğrafını gösterebilmek için bunları cihazındaki widget deposunda şifresiz tutar. Uygulama kilidini açarsan ya da o yazıyı kilitlersen widget'ta hiçbir yazı içeriği görünmez ve bu kopya silinir.

Fotoğraflar ve kamera

Fotoğraf arşivin yalnız cihazında, o günün fotoğraflarını sana göstermek için okunur. Bir fotoğrafı yazına eklemedikçe hiçbir şey yüklenmez; eklenen fotoğraf yazınla aynı şekilde şifrelenir.

Uygulamanın içinden kamerayla çektiğin fotoğraf, izin verirsen fotoğraf arşivine de kaydedilir; oradaki kopya Fotoğraflar uygulamasının kendi ayarlarına göre durur. Yaptığın gökyüzü posterlerini de istersen arşivine kaydedebilirsin.

Seçimine bağlı olanlar (başta kapalı)

  • Hava durumu ve yer: açarsan, yeni bir yazıya başlarken yaklaşık konumun (yaklaşık bir kilometreye yuvarlanır) yalnız o anki hava durumunu sormak (Apple WeatherKit) ve bulunduğun yerin adını bulmak (Apple'ın coğrafi kodlaması) için Apple'a gider. Koordinat saklanmaz; yer adı ve hava, yazının içinde şifreli durur.
  • Apple Sağlık: açarsan, seçtiğin his her yazı için bir "anlık duygu" kaydı olarak Sağlık uygulamasına yazılır. Bu veri cihazındaki Sağlık'ta kalır; kilitli yazılar yazılmaz.
  • Bildirimler: hatırlatmalar cihazında kurulur ve hiçbir zaman yazının metnini göstermez. Hatırlatma, o gün çektiğin bir fotoğrafı arşivinden küçük olarak gösterebilir; bunu Hatırlatma ayarlarındaki "Bugünün fotoğrafları" ile kapatabilirsin.
  • Face ID: uygulama kilidi Apple'ın cihaz üzerindeki doğrulamasını kullanır; yüz verisine erişmeyiz.

Privacy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Star Journal is a private journal. There is no account, no login and no advertising. We cannot read what you write.

The app uses no usage analytics, crash reporting or advertising tools. This page is also our notice under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Türkiye's data protection law (KVKK): what leaves your device, why, and your rights.

Who is responsible

This app and this page are made and published by Boran Parmaksız (Türkiye), the controller of your personal data. For any privacy question or request: boranparmaksiz06@gmail.com.

What you write

Titles, text, tags, moods and attached photos are encrypted on your iPhone with AES-GCM before they are stored. The key stays on your device and in your iCloud Keychain; we have no copy. With sync on, the encrypted data goes only to the private database of your own iCloud account. If you lose your recovery key, we cannot open your entries either.

The one exception is the Memory widget: while the app lock is off, the Home Screen Memory widget keeps the title, the first lines and a small photo of one past day unencrypted in the widget store on your device, so it can show them. Turn the app lock on, or lock that entry, and the widget shows no entry content and that copy is removed.

Photos and camera

Your photo library is read only on your device, to show you the photos of a given day. Nothing is uploaded unless you attach a photo to an entry; an attached photo is encrypted like the entry.

A photo you take with the camera inside the app is also saved to your photo library if you allow it; that copy follows the Photos app's own settings. You can also save the sky posters you make to your library.

Optional (off until you turn them on)

  • Weather and place: when on, starting a new entry sends your approximate location (rounded to about a kilometre) to Apple only to ask for the current weather (WeatherKit) and the name of the place you are in (Apple's geocoder). Coordinates are never stored; the place name and weather are kept encrypted inside the entry.
  • Apple Health: when on, the mood you pick is written to Health as a momentary "state of mind" for each entry. It stays in Health on your device; locked entries are never written.
  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled on your device and never show the text of an entry. A reminder can show a small photo you took that day from your library; turn this off with "Today's photos" in the reminder settings.
  • Face ID: the app lock uses Apple's on-device authentication; we never see face data.

What leaves your device

Apart from your encrypted entries, only the following leaves your device. For each one: what it is, why, the legal basis, who receives it and how long it is kept.

1. Purchases and subscription status (RevenueCat)

  • What: a random user ID the app creates when installed, the device ID Apple gives this app (IDFV), your App Store purchase history (which plan, when, trial or not, renewing or not), your store country, device model, iOS and app version, and language preferences. As with any connection, your IP address also reaches RevenueCat.
  • Why: to know whether Premium is on, to verify purchases and restores, to see trial and subscription numbers in aggregate, and to prevent abuse.
  • Legal basis: performing the contract you enter when you buy Premium (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)); our legitimate interest in aggregate sales numbers and abuse prevention (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Who: RevenueCat, Inc. (United States), which processes this on our behalf and on our instructions, and checks purchases with Apple's servers.
  • How long: as long as we use RevenueCat. Write to us to have your record deleted.

It is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking: no name, e-mail or Apple Account is added. In RevenueCat's dashboard we see a random ID and a purchase record, never your name. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

2. Payment (Apple)

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are made through Apple. Apple takes the payment; we never see your card, your name or your Apple Account. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy.

3. Weather and place (Apple, only if you turn it on)

  • What: your location, rounded to about a kilometre, when you start a new entry.
  • Why: to add the current weather and the name of the place to your entry.
  • Legal basis: providing a feature you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). Turn it off at any time in the app's settings or in iOS location settings.
  • Who: Apple (WeatherKit and geocoding). Your location never reaches us.
  • How long: the app never stores the coordinates; Apple's side follows Apple's policy.

4. If you write to us

  • What: your e-mail address, your message and anything you attach.
  • Why: to answer you, fix your problem and handle data protection requests.
  • Legal basis: answering your request (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)); our legal obligation for data protection requests (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Who: our mailbox is on Gmail, run by Google (United States).
  • How long: deleted at the latest one year after the matter is closed.

5. This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., United States). There are no cookies, analytics, external fonts or scripts. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security when it serves the pages, under GitHub's privacy statement. Legal basis: legitimate interest in running the site securely (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

How it is collected

Electronically: purchase data automatically through RevenueCat's software kit when the app opens; weather and place only when you turn them on, through Apple's services in iOS; e-mail data when you write to us; site logs on GitHub's servers when you open a page.

Is any of this required?

Only the purchase data: without it the app cannot sell or check Premium. Everything optional above works only if you turn it on, and the app works without it.

Automated decisions

The app suggests a mood from your words using word lists on your device. Nothing is sent, and no decision with legal or similarly significant effects is made about you.

International transfers

We are based in Türkiye, outside the EU and the EEA, and the services above run outside both:

  • RevenueCat, Inc. (United States): under RevenueCat's Data Processing Addendum, which includes the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (controller to processor) and the UK Addendum.
  • Apple: payments under Apple's own terms; weather and place only if you turn them on; iCloud sync to your own iCloud account, encrypted.
  • Google (United States): e-mails you send us.
  • GitHub, Inc. (United States): this site's visit logs.
